Questions

How many books can I have out at once?

Three. Swap them as often as you like — there's no monthly cap on how many you get through, only on how many you hold at a time.

What if I'm slow, or I stop reading for a while?

That's fine. There are no due dates and no late fees. We built this for people who are still finding their way back to reading, and a deadline is the fastest way to make that feel like homework.

What if I want to keep a book?

Members pay $2 for any book they want to keep, instead of the usual $3. Just tell us when you swap.

Can I cancel?

Any time, no notice needed. Bring your books back whenever you next pass by.

Where do I pick up and drop off?

930 Roosevelt, Room 226, Irvine, CA 92620. Same place as every other Lexi pick-up.

What happens to the money?

It goes straight back into the shelf — buying the titles people ask for that nobody has donated, and keeping books free for readers who can't pay.
